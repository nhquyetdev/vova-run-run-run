❓ How to play

🏃 Move with ← → ↑ ↓ / WASD, or drag anywhere on the screen like a joystick. Moving in any direction earns XP.

⬆ Level up and you run faster. How far can you go? Nobody knows… yet.

⭐ Grab the glowing star dust for bonus XP.

🤖 AUTO (Q) keeps running for you.

❓ Every place, speed and phase is a secret until you reach it.

🏆 Challenges (Menu): race animals, machines and light, escape planets, stars and black holes. Dodge with ↑↓.

← Running back takes you back: earlier places, earlier phases.

👥 In a room (up to 8) arrows show where friends are. Run toward them — you speed up through places you know and brake when you arrive. 🤝 TEAM pace keeps you beside slower friends.
